About
Documentary habits, pointed at your brand.
Patrick Linehan is an Emmy and Clio-award winning director specializing in commercial, branded content, narrative, and music videos. He's worked with brands like Red Bull, Oatly, and Marshall, and directed for hundreds of musical artists from around the world including WHATMORE, Elmiene, Mark Ambor, Kevin Atwater, Royel Otis, Calum Scott, Ayumu Imazu, and Maggie Rogers across narrative pieces, live sets, and music videos.

He got his start producing documentaries for ABC News, where he won an Emmy for Uvalde: 365, a year-long look into the aftermath of the 2022 school shooting in south Texas. Since redirecting his focus onto directing in 2023, he has worked on over 200 projects, amassing more than 150 million views across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. The scripted-doc series he made alongside WHATMORE won a Clio award for outstanding music marketing.
