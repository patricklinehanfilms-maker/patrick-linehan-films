Citi Bike Surfer

Ride 7th Avenue from Central Park South down to 14th St. Cars and cabs come up fast from behind (watch for the arrows at the bottom), crosstown traffic cuts across at every street, parked car doors swing open across the bike lane, and people park wherever they want. It gets faster and busier the further downtown you go.

On a phone, tilt left and right to steer. On a computer, use the arrow keys. One crash and the ride is over.
